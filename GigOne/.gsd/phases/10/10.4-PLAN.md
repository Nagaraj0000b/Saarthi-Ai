# Plan 10.4: Android UI Update (Vehicle to Skill Sets)

## Objective
Update the Android frontend to allow users to select their `Skill Sets` rather than their `Vehicle` during onboarding, profile editing, and anywhere else vehicle information is gathered or displayed.

## Context
- .gsd/DECISIONS.md (Review the 3 Categories and 8 Skills mapping)
- android/ (Kotlin viewmodels and screens)
