# Plan 10.1: ML Dataset Redesign (Skill Sets) & Retraining

## Objective
Convert the dataset generator and ML model away from "vehicles" to 8 boolean "skill sets". Expand the job types to 5 distinct categories, run generator, and retrain the model.

## Context
- .gsd/DECISIONS.md
- ml_engine/generate_dataset.py
- ml_engine/main.py (For understanding how the outputs are consumed)

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Update generate_dataset.py with new Skill Sets</name>
  <files>ml_engine/generate_dataset.py</files>
  <action>
    - Remove 'vehicle' and 'COMPATIBILITY' (lines 79-102 approx).
    - Introduce 5 `JOB_TYPES`: 
      1. 'Ride Hailing (Cab + Bike)'

      3. 'Delivery Workers in General'
      4. 'On Demand Home Based Services'
